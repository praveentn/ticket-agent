# Vendor Payments and Purchase Orders (Finance Internal)

## Payment terms
Standard vendor payment terms are net 45 days from invoice receipt. Early payment discounts above 2% should be taken when cash position allows.

## Purchase orders
All purchases above USD 10,000 need a purchase order before the invoice is issued. Invoices without a matching PO are placed on hold.
