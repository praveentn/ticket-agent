# Corporate Card Guidelines

## Eligibility
Employees who travel more than 4 times a year can apply for a corporate card with manager approval. The standard monthly limit is USD 5,000.

## Use
The corporate card is for business expenses only. Personal use, even if repaid, is a policy violation. Reconcile card transactions in the expense system within 30 days of the statement date.

## Lost card
Report a lost or stolen card to the card issuer immediately using the number on the back of your card, then notify Finance.
