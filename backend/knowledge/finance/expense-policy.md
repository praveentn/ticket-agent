# Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expense claims
Submit expense claims in the expense system within 30 days of the expense date with an itemized receipt for every item above USD 25. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed. Claims above USD 1,000 require manager approval before Finance review.

## Reimbursement timing
Approved claims are paid in the next payroll cycle. Finance reviews claims within 5 business days of manager approval.

## Meals
Meal allowances while travelling are up to USD 75 per day in the US and Western Europe and up to USD 40 per day elsewhere. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events.

## Lodging and flights
Book hotels and flights through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Hotel rates should stay within the city cap shown in the portal.

## Ground transport and mileage
Taxis and ride-share are reimbursable for business trips. Personal car use is reimbursed at USD 0.67 per mile or the local statutory rate.
