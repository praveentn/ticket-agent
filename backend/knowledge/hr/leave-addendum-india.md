# Leave Addendum - India

## Entitlement
Employees in India receive 21 days of earned leave, 12 days of sick leave and 6 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be combined with earned leave for more than 7 consecutive days.

## Public holidays
India offices observe 10 fixed public holidays plus 2 floating holidays of the employee's choice, which must be selected in Workday by January 31.
