# Benefits Guide - United States

## Annual leave
US employees receive 20 days of PTO per year plus 11 company holidays.

## Health insurance
Medical, dental and vision coverage starts on your first day. Open enrollment runs every November; outside it, you can change plans only within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## 401(k)
The company matches 100% of the first 4% of salary you contribute, vesting immediately.
