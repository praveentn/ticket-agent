# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule
Employees in hybrid roles work from the office at least 2 days per week; teams agree their anchor days. Fully remote arrangements need manager and HR approval.

## Working from another country
Working from a country other than your employment country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year, and needs pre-approval through the HR portal because of tax and immigration rules.

## Home office allowance
Hybrid and remote employees can claim a one-time home office allowance of USD 500 (or local equivalent) for a desk, chair or monitor, through the expense system under the category "equipment".
