# Compensation Bands and Merit Cycle (Managers)

## Merit cycle
The annual merit cycle runs in March. Managers receive a merit budget of 4.5% of their team's base salary. Increases above 10% for an individual require HR Business Partner approval.

## Promotion increases
Promotions carry a standard increase of 8 to 12 percent and are processed in the April and October payroll cycles.
