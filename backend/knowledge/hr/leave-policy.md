# Global Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue annual leave monthly; the yearly entitlement depends on country (see your country addendum). Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next calendar year and must be used by March 31. Leave requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for absences longer than 3 days.

## Sick leave
Notify your manager before the start of your working day. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over.

## Parental leave
Birth parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave and non-birth parents receive 16 weeks, which can be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Notify HR at least 8 weeks before the planned start date.

## Approvals
Leave of up to 10 days is approved by your manager. Leave longer than 10 consecutive days also needs HR review to plan coverage. Unpaid leave always requires HR approval.
