# Laptops, Peripherals and Hardware Support

## Standard equipment
Every employee receives a laptop on a 3-year refresh cycle, one external monitor, a headset, a keyboard and a mouse. Engineers may request a second monitor without manager approval.

## Broken or damaged laptop
Back up your work to OneDrive, then raise a ticket. Walk-in Tech Bars in every major office swap hardware the same day. For remote employees, a replacement laptop is shipped within 2 business days and you return the old one with the prepaid label.

## Slow laptop
Restart at least once a week so updates install. Check Software Center for pending updates. If the laptop is more than 3 years old, it is eligible for early refresh.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen device immediately as a P1 security incident so it can be remotely wiped. Also file a police report for theft and attach the reference to the ticket.
