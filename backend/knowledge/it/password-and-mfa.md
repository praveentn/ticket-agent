# Passwords, Account Lockouts and MFA

## Password requirements
Corporate SSO passwords must be at least 14 characters and are valid for 180 days. You cannot reuse any of your last 10 passwords. You will get reminder emails 14, 7 and 1 day before expiry.

## Resetting a forgotten password
Use the self-service reset at https://sso.acme.example/reset or ask the support assistant to send a reset link. The link is sent to your registered mobile number and expires in 15 minutes. IT staff will never ask for your password or MFA code.

## Account lockouts
Your account locks for 30 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. You can wait for it to unlock automatically or ask the support assistant to unlock it immediately. Repeated lockouts often come from an old password saved on a phone mail app or a mapped network drive; update or remove the saved password on those devices.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
MFA through the Microsoft Authenticator app is mandatory for all employees. If you get a new phone, re-register MFA at https://mfa.acme.example before wiping your old phone. If you lost your phone, the Service Desk can issue a temporary access pass valid for 8 hours after verifying your identity by video call with your manager present.
