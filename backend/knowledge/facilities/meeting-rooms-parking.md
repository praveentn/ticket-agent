# Meeting Rooms and Parking

## Meeting rooms
Meeting rooms can be booked through Outlook or the support assistant up to 30 days in advance. Bookings not checked in within 10 minutes of the start time are released automatically. Rooms for more than 20 people need Workplace Services approval.

## Parking
Parking permits are allocated quarterly, with priority for employees with accessibility needs and car-pool groups. Visitor parking must be booked by the host at least 24 hours in advance. EV charging bays are limited to 4 hours per day.
