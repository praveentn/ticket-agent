# Office Services and Building Support

## Access badges
New badges are issued at reception on your first day. Lost badges are deactivated immediately when reported; a replacement badge costs nothing for the first loss in a year and is ready the next business day.

## Building issues
Report temperature, lighting, cleaning or maintenance issues through a ticket. Workplace Services responds within 4 business hours. For water leaks, electrical hazards or anything unsafe, call building security at extension 5555 immediately in addition to raising a ticket.

## Desks
Hot desks are booked through the Workplace app up to 14 days in advance. Ergonomic assessments and equipment such as standing desks can be requested through Workplace Services.
